DATA + CODE OWNERSHIP

Clear lines.
From day one.

Using the system does not give SmartInterop ownership of your business information. Deploying the system does not transfer ownership of SmartInterop’s software.

01 Your data
You own it.

You retain ownership of the data, documents, prompts, business rules, and other materials you provide, together with your rights in workflow outputs.

02 SmartInterop software
SmartInterop owns it.

SmartInterop retains ownership of its platform, agent framework, source code, reusable components, deployment tools, and generalized improvements.

03 Customer-hosted use
The delivered version is licensed.

The $2,000 deployment fee includes a continuing, non-exclusive license to use the delivered version on the agreed customer-controlled system. It does not transfer copyright or source-code ownership.

04 SaaS access
Access follows the subscription.

The subscription provides the right to use the managed service while the subscription is active. It does not transfer the service or its source code.

Need different ownership terms? A source-code transfer or customer-owned custom component must be identified, separately priced, and agreed in writing before development.
